YT Research Pipeline — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 11, 2026

YT Research Pipeline is a private, single-user research tool that monitors public YouTube channel feeds for personal study of investments, economics, and crypto.

What the app accesses

The app requests the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly only, used to read the operator's YouTube subscription list to monitor public channel feeds. It never accesses other Google services or modifies the account.

Terms

  • This is a personal, single-user research tool provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.
  • It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Output is analytical research material, not investment advice.
  • The app's handling of Google user data is described in its Privacy Policy.
  • Access may be revoked at any time at Google Security — third-party permissions.
